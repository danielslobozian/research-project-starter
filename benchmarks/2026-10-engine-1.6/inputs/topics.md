# Topics

The 55 casual questions of the benchmark: each is the message a person would type, written in the style of real public questions (Reddit, search queries), not copied from a specific post. The same text starts all three arms. T = everyday topics, S = scientific or technical topics. The cross-model rounds use T3, T9, T20, S13 and S23 (draw.json); Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna and GLM-5.3 Flash ran all 55.
